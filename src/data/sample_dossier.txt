conditions. The compound demonstrates selective binding affinity and a favourable
pharmacokinetic profile in preclinical and Phase I/II studies.

The benefit-risk balance is considered favourable for the proposed indication.

5.3 Clinical Study Reports

5.3.1 Reports of Biopharmaceutic Studies
5.3.1.1 Study NC-BA-001: Absolute Bioavailability Study
Open-label, 2-period crossover. N=12 healthy male volunteers.
Single oral dose 200 mg tablet vs single IV dose 50 mg. Absolute BA = 62.4%
(90% CI: 58.1–66.7%). PK well-characterised; no safety concerns.

5.3.1.2 Study NC-BA-002: Comparative BA / Bioequivalence
Bioequivalence confirmed for Phase II tablet vs to-be-marketed formulation
(Cmax 90% CI: 91.3–104.8%; AUC 90% CI: 93.2–107.1%). Both within 80–125% limits.

5.3.1.3 Study NC-FE-001: Food Effect Study
Fed/fasted crossover, n=16 healthy volunteers. High-fat meal delays Tmax by 1.5 h
and reduces Cmax by 18%. AUC unaffected. SmPC recommends administration without food.

5.3.2 Reports of Human Pharmacokinetic Studies
5.3.2.1 Study NC-PK-001: Single/Multiple Ascending Dose (SAD/MAD)
Phase I, healthy volunteers, n=42 (SAD cohorts: 25, 50, 100, 200, 400 mg; MAD:
100 and 200 mg once daily for 14 days). Dose-proportional PK. Steady state achieved
by Day 5. t½ = 8–10 h supporting once-daily dosing.

5.3.2.2 Study NC-DDI-001: Drug-Drug Interaction with CYP3A4 Inhibitor
Co-administration with ketoconazole 400 mg once daily for 7 days increased
NovaCept-200 AUC by 2.3-fold. Dose adjustment guidance included in SmPC.

5.3.2.3 Population PK Analysis
PopPK model developed using NONMEM (v7.5). Body weight and renal function identified
as significant covariates but do not require dose adjustment within proposed label.

5.3.3 Reports of Human Pharmacodynamic Studies
Pharmacodynamic data presented within the Phase II study report (Study NC-PH2-001).
Dose-response relationship confirmed. Exposure-response modelling supports 200 mg
once daily as optimal dose.

5.3.4 Reports of Efficacy and Safety Studies
5.3.4.1 Study NC-PH2-001: Phase II Randomised Controlled Trial
Design: 12-week, randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group.
Population: Adults (18–75 years) with moderate-to-severe chronic inflammatory
condition. N=186 randomised (93 NovaCept-200 200 mg once daily; 93 placebo).
Primary endpoint: Change from baseline in composite inflammatory score at Week 12.
Results: NovaCept-200 group: mean change −8.4 points (SD 3.2).
Placebo group: mean change −3.1 points (SD 3.0).
Difference: −5.3 points (95% CI: −6.5 to −4.1; p<0.001, MMRM analysis).
Response rate (≥50% improvement): 61.3% vs 29.0% (NovaCept-200 vs placebo; p<0.001).
Remission rate (score ≤2): 28.0% vs 9.7% (p=0.003).

Safety summary (Phase II): 186 patients received ≥1 dose (NovaCept-200: 93).
Treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs) ≥5%: headache 12.9%, nausea 9.7%,
fatigue 8.6%. No deaths. Two serious adverse events (not drug-related per investigator).
Two cases of ALT elevation >3×ULN, resolved on dose hold/reduction.
Discontinuations due to AEs: 4.3% (NovaCept-200) vs 2.2% (placebo).
